Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Short version: there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no advertising cookies. Test pages are made in your browser, and we don't collect your name, email or anything you type into the page.

What you type stays in your browser

Everything you pick or type to build a test page, including the technician name or footnote field, is used only to draw the page in your browser. None of it is sent to us or stored on our servers.

What we store in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) for two small conveniences. Neither value identifies you, and neither is sent anywhere:

  • pj_last_increment_at: when you last printed, so repeated prints don't inflate the counter.
  • pj_mobile_banner_dismissed: remembers that you closed the "best viewed on a computer" notice.

You can clear these at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

The global print counter

When you print, the site asks our server to add one to a shared, anonymous tally, which is kept by a counter service called CounterAPI. The request goes through our own server, so the counter service receives the "+1" but not your IP address, and nothing about who printed or what was printed is recorded.

Hosting and site statistics

PaperJammed.net is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Short-lived request logs may be used to fix problems.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see overall numbers such as page views, popular pages and referring sites. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and gives us only totals, not individual profiles.

Fonts and icons from other services

To load its styling, fonts and icons, the main page requests files from these providers. As with any web request, they receive your IP address and browser details:

  • Google Fonts (Google privacy policy)
  • cdnjs, run by Cloudflare, for the icons
  • the Tailwind CSS CDN, for styling

Advertising and affiliate links

The site doesn't currently show ads or use affiliate links. If that changes, this page will be updated before they go live to name the partners involved, describe any cookies they use, and explain your choices, including how to opt out of personalized ads.

Children

PaperJammed.net is a general-audience humor site. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Emailing us

If you email contact@paperjammed.net, we'll use your email address and message only to reply. We won't add you to a mailing list or share your address.

Your choices

You can use the site with local storage blocked or cleared; the print cooldown and the dismissed notice just won't be remembered. If you have a question about your data or want something deleted, email contact@paperjammed.net.

Changes to this policy

If what the site collects changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.
